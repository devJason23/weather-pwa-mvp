## Benefits grid

### Faster player development
When players can review what happened and get clear feedback, improvement happens faster. Film and stats shorten the gap between a mistake, the lesson, and the next better rep.

### Better basketball IQ
Great players do not just play hard. They learn to read the floor, understand spacing, recognize patterns, and make smarter decisions. Reviewing possessions helps players think the game at a higher level.

### Objective performance feedback
Emotion fades. Film does not. Stats do not. Players and coaches can work from a shared picture of what actually happened, which leads to more honest coaching and more productive growth.

### More accountability
When effort, execution, and results are visible, players learn to take ownership. Accountability is not punishment. It is a path to maturity, trust, and leadership.

### Stronger team learning
One game can teach an entire team. Film and stat review help groups understand tendencies, spacing, transition habits, shot quality, and defensive discipline in ways that normal post-game conversations cannot.

### Progress you can actually see
Development should be visible. Over time, players should be able to see growth in decision-making, consistency, shot selection, effort habits, and role execution. This platform helps make progress measurable.
